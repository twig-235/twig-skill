# Prompt scaffolds

## Full

Create an original adult game character in the requested role and palette. Full body, head and feet visible, signature weapon readable, environmental background, hand-painted 2D Korean dark-fantasy anime RPG style, no UI or text.

## Chat

Use the approved full illustration as the exact identity reference. Transparent character cutout from the top of the head to just above the knees. Keep adult proportions; exclude knees, shins, and boots. No background, matte, UI, or text.

## Icon

Use the approved identity. Square head-and-shoulders portrait with a readable face at 100 px. Quiet contrasting background allowed. No card frame, stars, level, name, class glyph, or selection mark.

## Grid

Use the approved identity in SD proportions. Exactly 4 × 4 transparent cells with rows ordered idle, attack, exploration/work, and hit. Four chronological frames per row. Keep all content within cell boundaries with transparent gutters.
