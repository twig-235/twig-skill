# Prompt scaffolds

## Full

Use the corresponding official full.png masters for rendering style, not pose or scenery. Choose a natural, varied camera angle, gaze, stance and setting. Create an original adult game character in the requested role and palette. Full body, head and feet visible, signature weapon readable, environmental background, hand-painted 2D Korean dark-fantasy anime RPG style, no UI or text.

## Chat

Use the approved full illustration as the exact identity reference and official chat.png masters for framing and style. Transparent character cutout from the top of the head to just above the knees. Keep adult proportions; exclude knees, shins, and boots. No background, matte, UI, or text.

## Icon

Use the approved identity and official icon.png masters for framing and style. Square head-and-shoulders portrait with a readable face at 100 px. Quiet contrasting background allowed. No card frame, stars, level, name, class glyph, or selection mark.

## Grid

Use the approved identity and official grid.png masters for SD proportion and style. Exactly 4 × 4 transparent cells with rows ordered idle, attack, exploration/work, and hit. Four chronological frames per row. Keep all content within cell boundaries with transparent gutters.
